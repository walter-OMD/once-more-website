What is GEO?

Generative engine optimisation (GEO) is the work of making your business easy for AI tools to find, trust and cite when they answer a question. Tools such as ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Google's AI Overviews now write a direct answer and name a handful of sources, instead of listing ten links. GEO is about being one of the sources they name.

You may also see it called AEO (answer engine optimisation), AI optimisation or LLM optimisation. The labels differ and the goal is the same. For a shorter explainer, see what GEO is and why it matters.

How is GEO different from SEO?

Less than the name suggests. Google's guidance on its generative AI features says they are rooted in its core ranking and quality systems, and from its perspective optimising for AI search is still SEO. Crawlable pages, useful content, a clear site structure and credible links help both.

What GEO adds is attention to how your business gets read, summarised and named inside an answer:

That is why we treat GEO as an extension of SEO. A business with a weak site gets little from GEO work alone.

How do ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews choose which sources to cite?

Most of these tools do not crawl the whole web live for every question. They lean on an existing search index, then pick from what they retrieve.

AI toolWhere it looksWhat that means for you
Google AI Overviews and AI ModeGoogle's Search index, using the same ranking and quality systemsYour page must be indexed and eligible to show with a snippet
ChatGPT (browsing) and Microsoft CopilotBing's indexBing visibility matters. Seer Interactive found 87% of ChatGPT citations matched Bing's top results, against 56% for Google

Google also says its AI features can run several related searches at once to answer one question, which Google calls query fan-out. A page that answers the related questions around a topic has more ways to be retrieved. Ranking gets you into the pool, and clear, original content is what gets you picked from it.

Why does GEO matter for Malaysian businesses?

  • The language mix. People search in English and Malay, sometimes within a single query, and for some audiences in Chinese. AI tools usually answer in the language of the question, so content in only one language leaves gaps.
  • Local intent. A search like "dental clinic in KL" is the kind AI tools answer with a short list of named businesses. Google says a Google Business Profile helps local businesses appear in AI responses, and our local SEO guide covers that side.
  • The shortlist effect. An AI answer does not hand buyers ten options to compare. It names a few. If you are not among them, you may lose the enquiry before anyone visits a website.
  • Room to move. In our experience, few Malaysian businesses have built deliberately for this yet, so the ones that do the work now stand out.

What should a GEO checklist include?

Google's guidance and our own client work point the same way: the basics matter more than tricks.

  • Be indexed and eligible. To appear in Google's AI features, a page must be indexed and eligible to show with a snippet, and your site must not have opted out of generative AI features in Search Console. Set up Bing Webmaster Tools too, because ChatGPT and Copilot lean on Bing.
  • Publish original content. Google says unique, non-commodity content likely matters more over time than any other tactic. That means first-hand experience, real data and real examples, not a rewrite of what everyone else already says.
  • Write clearly for readers. Put the direct answer near the top, use clear headings and short paragraphs, and add a table where it helps. That helps people and AI tools alike.
  • Make your business easy to identify. Use the same name, address, services and registration details everywhere, and keep your Google Business Profile and Bing Places listing complete. Organization and LocalBusiness schema is worth having, even though Google says it is not required for AI features.
  • Earn real mentions. Reviews, press, partner pages, directories and case studies that come from real relationships all count. Google warns that manufactured mentions are less useful than they look.
  • Say the same thing consistently. AI tools build their picture of a brand from repeated signals. If your edge is fast turnaround or bilingual support, describe it the same way on your service pages, FAQs, About page and third-party profiles.

What you can skip. For Google Search, Google says you can ignore llms.txt files and other special AI markup, chopping content into tiny pieces, rewriting pages just for AI, and chasing inauthentic mentions. It also warns that no third-party tool has access to its internal ranking or AI systems. If an agency sells any of these as the core of GEO, ask why. Google's guide to optimising for generative AI features sets this out in full.

How do you measure GEO results?

No tool gives a complete picture of AI visibility yet. What works in practice is a mix:

  • Search Console's Generative AI report. Google launched it in June 2026, and it is still in beta. It shows how often your pages appear in AI Overviews and AI Mode, by page, country, device and date. It reports impressions only, with no clicks or queries, and not every property has it yet.
  • Bing Webmaster Tools, AI Performance. Bing's public preview shows citations in Microsoft Copilot, AI summaries in Bing and select partner integrations, including which pages are cited and the phrases the AI used to find them.
  • Prompt testing. Pick 20 to 30 questions your customers would ask, run them monthly in ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Google, and log whether you are named and where.
  • Competitor benchmarking. Compare how often 3 to 5 competitors are named. Your AI competitors may differ from your Google competitors. A mention can also be positive, neutral or negative, so read how the AI describes you, not only how often. Semrush's guide to benchmarking brand mentions walks through this, and its free AI Search Visibility Checker gives a quick snapshot.
  • GA4 referrals. Watch visits from chatgpt.com, perplexity.ai and gemini.google.com.
  • Enquiries. This matters most. Citations only count if they lead to business.

Treat all of this as directional. AI answers vary by person and by day, and the tools sample them.

How do you choose a GEO agency in Malaysia?

Ask these before you sign:

  • Do they do SEO properly? GEO without SEO fundamentals stalls.
  • Can they show Malaysian results with real numbers? Ask for data you can check in Search Console. Ours are on our case studies page.
  • Can they explain where AI tools get their sources? A clear answer beats talk of secret algorithms.
  • Are they honest about measurement? They should say what can and cannot be measured. No third-party tool sees inside Google's systems.
  • Do they avoid guarantees? Nobody can promise a citation or a position.
  • Will they tell you what to skip? A good agency can name the tactics it would not spend your money on.
  • Is the reporting clear, and the contract fair? You should be able to explain the report to your boss, and exit if it turns out to be vague.
  • Do they show up in AI answers themselves? Ask ChatGPT or Gemini about GEO agencies in Malaysia and see who is named.

Google also publishes guidance on evaluating third-party SEO advice, which applies equally to GEO.

WSPACE is a coworking operator in Kuala Lumpur. We structured their content so it could be read cleanly and cited by AI answer engines, alongside their SEO. Over the engagement their search clicks rose 284% and organic traffic rose 216.86%, and they told us the enquiries were arriving from proper company domains. The full story is in our WSPACE case study.

Search Console's Generative AI report adds a view of the AI side. Between 3 July and 1 October 2026, WSPACE's pages appeared 4.32K times in Google's AI features, roughly 47 appearances a day, with the daily count peaking at around 120 at the end of the period.

Impressions count appearances, and the report has no click data. It also covers Google's AI features, not ChatGPT or Perplexity. We treat it as one signal alongside traffic and enquiry quality. FAQ

Common questions

What is GEO and is it different from SEO?

GEO gets your business cited inside AI-written answers. It extends SEO rather than replacing it, since AI tools draw on an existing search index and Google itself treats optimising for AI search as SEO.

How much do GEO services cost in Malaysia?

It depends on your site's starting point, its size, the languages you need, how competitive your market is and how much content is involved. We scope after looking at your site, and the first step is a free SEO audit.

Can a Malaysian business appear in ChatGPT answers? How long does it take?

Yes, if your pages are indexed, ranking reasonably well and clear enough to be quoted. Early movement usually takes three to six months, and nobody can promise a specific citation.

Do I still need SEO if I do GEO?

Yes. The index AI tools read from is built on SEO signals, so the two work best together.

What does a GEO agency actually do?

It audits your visibility on Google, Bing and in AI answers, fixes technical and business-detail issues, restructures and writes original content, earns real third-party mentions and reports on citations and enquiries.

How do I know if my website is cited by AI engines?

Check Search Console's Generative AI report and Bing Webmaster Tools' AI Performance report, test a fixed set of customer questions monthly in the main AI tools, and watch referral traffic from them in GA4.
