What is generative engine optimisation (GEO) in Malaysia?

Guide

Updated October 2026

Short answer

Generative engine optimisation (GEO) is the work of making your business easy for AI tools such as ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews to find, trust and cite. Google treats it as part of SEO, because its AI features use the same Search index and ranking systems. In Malaysia, that means strong SEO in English and Malay, plus original, clearly written content that AI tools can quote.

What is GEO?

GEO is the work of making your business easy for AI tools to find, trust and cite. Tools like ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Google's AI Overviews now write a direct answer and name a few sources, instead of listing ten links. GEO is about being one of the sources they name.

You may also see it called AEO, AI optimisation or LLM optimisation. Same goal, different labels.

How is GEO different from SEO?

Less than the name suggests. Google says its AI features are rooted in its core ranking and quality systems, so from its perspective optimising for AI search is still SEO.

That is why we treat GEO as an extension of SEO. A weak site gets little from GEO work alone.

How do ChatGPT, Gemini and AI Overviews choose which sources to cite?

Most do not crawl the web live for every question. They retrieve from an existing search index, then pick from what comes back.

Ranking gets you into the pool. Clear, original content gets you picked from it. Google also runs several related searches at once for a single question, which it calls query fan-out, so pages that cover the related questions have more ways to be found.

Why does GEO matter for Malaysian businesses?

01

The language mix

People search in English and Malay, sometimes in one query. AI tools answer in the language asked, so single-language content leaves gaps.

02

Local intent

A search like "dental clinic in KL" gets a short list of named businesses. A complete Google Business Profile helps you make it. See our local SEO guide.

03

The shortlist effect

AI answers name a few options, not ten. Miss the list and you can lose the enquiry before anyone visits a website.

04

Room to move

In our experience few Malaysian businesses have built for this yet, so early movers stand out.

What should a GEO checklist include?

01

Be indexed and eligible

Pages must be indexed and eligible to show with a snippet, and your site must not have opted out of AI features in Search Console. Add Bing Webmaster Tools too, since ChatGPT and Copilot lean on Bing.

02

Publish original content

Google says unique, non-commodity content matters more over time than any other tactic. Use first-hand experience, real data and real examples.

03

Write clearly for readers

Direct answer up top, clear headings, short paragraphs, a table where it helps. People and AI tools both benefit.

04

Make your business easy to identify

Same name, address and services everywhere, with complete Google Business Profile and Bing Places listings. Schema helps, though Google says it is not required for AI features.

05

Earn real mentions

Reviews, press, partner pages and case studies from real relationships. Manufactured mentions are less useful than they look.

06

Say the same thing everywhere

AI tools build a picture of your brand from repeated signals. Describe your edge the same way on service pages, FAQs and third-party profiles.

What you can skip

For Google Search, Google says you can ignore llms.txt files and special AI markup, chopping content into tiny pieces, rewriting pages just for AI, and chasing inauthentic mentions. If an agency sells these as the core of GEO, ask why. Google's guide to optimising for generative AI features sets it out in full.

How do you measure GEO results?

No tool gives a complete picture of AI visibility yet. A mix works best:

MeasureWhat it showsThe catch
Search Console, Generative AI reportHow often your pages appear in AI Overviews and AI Mode, by page, country, device and dateBeta, impressions only, and not on every property yet
Bing Webmaster Tools, AI PerformanceCitations in Copilot, Bing's AI summaries and partner integrations, with the pages citedPublic preview, Microsoft surfaces only
Prompt testingWhether you are named when 20 to 30 customer questions are run monthly in ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and GoogleAnswers vary by person and by day
Competitor benchmarkingHow often 3 to 5 competitors are named, and how the AI describes youA count shows how often, not why, and a mention can be negative
GA4 referralsVisits from chatgpt.com, perplexity.ai and gemini.google.comOnly counts people who click through
EnquiriesWhether any of it brings businessSlowest to show, and the one that matters most

Treat all of it as directional.

How do you choose a GEO agency in Malaysia?

Look for

  • SEO done properly, because GEO without it stalls
  • Malaysian results with numbers you can check in Search Console. See our case studies
  • A clear account of where AI tools get their sources
  • Honesty about what can and cannot be measured
  • Clear reporting and a fair contract with an exit

Walk away if they

  • Guarantee a citation or a position
  • Sell llms.txt, special markup or bought mentions as the core of GEO
  • Quote a fixed price before seeing your site
  • Claim a tool sees inside Google's systems, which Google says none does

A quick test: ask ChatGPT or Gemini about GEO agencies in Malaysia and see who gets named. Google also publishes guidance on evaluating third-party SEO advice.

FAQ

Common questions

What is GEO and is it different from SEO?

GEO gets your business cited inside AI-written answers. It extends SEO rather than replacing it, since AI tools draw on an existing search index and Google itself treats optimising for AI search as SEO.

How much do GEO services cost in Malaysia?

It depends on your site's starting point, its size, the languages you need, how competitive your market is and how much content is involved. We scope after looking at your site, and the first step is a free SEO audit.

Can a Malaysian business appear in ChatGPT answers? How long does it take?

Yes, if your pages are indexed, ranking reasonably well and clear enough to be quoted. Early movement usually takes three to six months, and nobody can promise a specific citation.

Do I still need SEO if I do GEO?

Yes. The index AI tools read from is built on SEO signals, so the two work best together.

What does a GEO agency actually do?

It audits your visibility on Google, Bing and in AI answers, fixes technical and business-detail issues, restructures and writes original content, earns real third-party mentions and reports on citations and enquiries.

How do I know if my website is cited by AI engines?

Check Search Console's Generative AI report and Bing Webmaster Tools' AI Performance report, test a fixed set of customer questions monthly in the main AI tools, and watch referral traffic from them in GA4.
